## 🧩 Overview
**The Dungeon of Duality** is a puzzle-based dungeon crawler centered on solving challenges and escaping a dark, mysterious dungeon by **shifting between two realms** — the **Light Realm** and the **Shadow Realm**.  

Each realm has subtle differences that change the environment and reveal new paths or clues. The player must use this ability to avoid enemies, uncover secrets, and ultimately **escape the dungeon**.  

Victory is achieved when the player reaches the final exit gate. Losing all lives results in a **game over**.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay
The Dungeon of Duality blends **puzzle-solving** and **obstacle navigation** with **realm-shifting mechanics**.

Players explore rooms filled with traps, enemies, and puzzles that can only be solved by viewing the dungeon from both realms.  
As progression continues, the puzzles grow in complexity, requiring timing, observation, and creative thinking.

Throughout the dungeon, players can:
- **Collect Dark Orbs** to extend time in the Shadow Realm.  
- **Find throwable items** like rocks or bottles to distract enemies.  
- **Push and interact** with objects to solve pressure-plate puzzles.
